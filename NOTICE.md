# Notice

This repository evaluates FusionCore by Manan Kharwar:

- Paper: M. Kharwar, "FusionCore: A 23-State Unscented Kalman Filter for
  IMU, Wheel Encoder, GPS, and Visual SLAM Fusion in ROS 2,"
  arXiv:2605.25239, 2026.
- Software: https://github.com/manankharwar/fusioncore, Apache License 2.0.

Modifications to FusionCore are published separately, with the upstream
licence and a statement of changes, on branch `verification` of
https://github.com/rustamoz/fusioncore.

Evaluation used the University of Michigan North Campus Long-Term dataset:
N. Carlevaris-Bianco, A. K. Ushani and R. M. Eustice, "University of
Michigan North Campus long-term vision and lidar dataset," The
International Journal of Robotics Research, 35(9), 1023-1035, 2016.
The dataset is released under the Open Database License. No dataset files,
and no trajectories derived from them, are included in this repository.
